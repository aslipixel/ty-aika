# /brag plan — Työaika (ty-aika)

**What:** A phone-first PWA for logging work hours (Finnish UI). Type "0700" → 07:00, see the day and the week total instantly.
**For:** Hourly workers who need their weekly hours without a spreadsheet.
**Hook:** "Montako tuntia tällä viikolla?" with a 0 h counter.
**Angle:** The fastest timesheet you'll ever fill. Real app, real interactions, placeholder entries.
**Tone:** default (punchy, playful, clean). Identity: violet #7c5cff → pink #ff6fb1 → #ffb86b gradient, Inter.
**Data:** all entries are fictional placeholders ("Asennus", "Toimisto", …). The TESTIVERSIO badge is hidden.
**Music:** 120 BPM (beat 0.5 s, bar 2 s). All cuts on bar lines.
